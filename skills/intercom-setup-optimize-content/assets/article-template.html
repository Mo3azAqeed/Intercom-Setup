Use this article to understand how Firecrawl rate limits work, find out why you are getting a 429 error, and fix it. Rate limits apply to every Firecrawl plan, and the limit for your team depends on which plan you are on.

What are Firecrawl rate limits?

Firecrawl rate limits cap how many API requests your team can make per minute. Concurrency limits cap how many jobs can run at the same time. Both limits are set by your plan.

Note: Rate limits apply per team, so every API key on the same team shares the same counters.

Why am I getting a 429 error?

A 429 (Too Many Requests) error means your team has gone over its Firecrawl rate limit or concurrency limit for your plan.

How do I fix a 429 error?

To fix a Firecrawl 429 error, retry the request with exponential backoff:

  1. Wait 1 second, then retry the request.
  2. If you get another 429 error, wait 2 seconds, then retry.
  3. If you get another 429 error, wait 4 seconds, then retry.

When the retry succeeds, the request returns your data as normal. If you still get 429 errors after retrying, upgrade your plan or contact help@example.com to ask about a custom plan.
